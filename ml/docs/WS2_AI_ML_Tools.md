> **Disclosure (methodology honesty).** UC-I engines are deliberately **deterministic,
> physics-based and explainable** for the PoC: under-keel clearance and tidal windows
> are safety-of-navigation computations where auditable formulas are preferred over
> black-box ML, and the shared corpus does not contain enough labelled vessel-call
> history to train credible arrival-time models. Where a component is rule-based, the
> "Model Weights" column points to its versioned configuration (coefficients) instead
> of learned weights; the production path to learned models is stated in each row.
